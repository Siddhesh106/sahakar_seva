# SahakarSeva — Product Requirements Document (PRD)
### SIH26089: Cooperative Gig Services Platform for Household & Community Services
**Ministry of Cooperation Initiative**

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Modern gig platforms extract 20–35% commissions from blue-collar service workers while functioning as opaque black boxes for job matching. **SahakarSeva** transforms household and community service delivery into a worker-owned cooperative model. By legally anchoring the platform within local Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) and Urban Cooperative Societies:
1. **Platform Fee is Capped at 8.5%** — direct worker payouts exceed 91.5%.
2. **Transparent Fair-Match Engine** replaces secretive algorithmic throttling with open, explainable job scoring (Proximity 40%, Rating 30%, Fair-Turn Idle Priority 30%).
3. **Net Surplus is Re-Distributed** quarterly as member dividends.
4. **Institutional Safety Net**: Automatic linking to the national e-Shram registry and PACS advance credit against pending earnings.

---

## 2. Target Personas

### 🧑 Customer (Urban Household)
- **Pain Points**: Sudden household failures (leaking tap, shorted fan), unpredictable pricing, untrusted gig workers.
- **Needs**: Instant discovery, clear fixed pricing, verified worker identity, live tracking, digital payments.

### 👷 Worker-Member (Electrician, Plumber, Cleaner, Cook)
- **Pain Points**: High commission cuts, punitive platform de-activations, zero social security, credit denial from banks.
- **Needs**: Transparent assignment, prompt wallet payouts, cooperative membership, emergency micro-credit, accident insurance via e-Shram.

### 🏛️ Cooperative Society Admin
- **Responsibilities**: Worker KYC verification, community trust, dispute adjudication, quarterly dividend payouts.

## 3. Core Feature Matrix

| Feature Area | Description | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **AI Request Parser** | Converts unstructured natural language (`"bedroom fan not spinning"`) into structured service specifications. | Zero friction booking for seniors & non-technical users. |
| **Fair-Match Engine** | 4-step candidate evaluation based on distance (40%), rating (30%), and idle time (30%). | Eliminates worker starvation and monopolization. |
| **Explainable Scoring** | 3-bar visual breakdown on every job offer screen. | 100% worker transparency and algorithmic trust. |
| **Cooperative Fee Split** | Fixed 8.5% fee; 91.5% credited immediately to worker wallet. | Maximizes blue-collar income. |
| **e-Shram & PACS Gateway** | UAN issuance and micro-advances against earnings. | Institutional social protection. |
| **WhatsApp Bot Second Door** | Complete job acceptance and completion flow via SMS/WhatsApp keywords (`1`, `2`, `START`, `DONE`). | Low-literacy and low-bandwidth accessibility. |
